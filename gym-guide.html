Your 4-Day Gym Split

Chest and triceps, back and biceps, legs and hips, then shoulders and arms—with core work and short cardio finishes. Start light and build control before adding weight.

Current weekly plan: Monday — Chest & Triceps · Tuesday — Back & Biceps · Thursday — Legs, Hips & Arms · Saturday — Shoulders, Arms & Core. Finish each session with a short 10–15 minute easy run or brisk incline walk. Keep 2–3 good reps in reserve and stop if you feel pain.

How to use this guide

  1. Choose a workout day from the tabs, or search by exercise or muscle.
  2. Look at the red areas in the small body drawing. They show the main muscles used, not every muscle involved.
  3. Read the form cues, then tap Watch video for a demonstration.
  4. Begin with a light load. Move slowly, keep the motion controlled, and ask gym staff to check your form if unsure.
  5. When every set reaches the top of its rep range with clean form, raise the load by a small amount next time.

The body drawings are simple learning diagrams, not medical or exact anatomical images. Muscle use varies with grip, setup, and body position.

Warm up for 5–8 minutes. Rest about 2 minutes after big lifts and 60–90 seconds after smaller movements. The short cardio finish supports fitness and calorie expenditure; sustainable weight loss also depends on food intake, sleep and consistency.
